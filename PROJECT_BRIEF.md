# Derma Delight — Preliminary Product Brief

## Purpose
Create a premium Kenyan single-brand e-commerce experience for adult skincare and children's body/hair care. The store is owned and operated by Derma Delight, not a marketplace.

## Brand
- Name: Derma Delight
- Positioning: Science for Radiant Skin
- Website: dermadelight.co.ke (planned)
- Email: info@dermadelight.co.ke
- Phone: +254 720 815 270
- Location: Ruiru, Kenya
- Manufacturing: Derma Delight works with licensed manufacturers. Do **not** describe the brand as owning a factory or manufacturing products itself.

## Experience
Homepage: announcement, navigation, editorial hero, Adults/Kids collection cards, product grid, brand values, story, closing call-to-action and footer.

Customer flows (future): browse → view product → add to cart → choose delivery → pay → receive confirmation → track order.

Admin flows (future): sign in → manage products, prices, images, stock → process orders → review sales.

## Current limitations
This scaffold is a design/interaction preview, not a functioning production shop. It does not collect payment, send orders, store customer data on a server or have live inventory.

## Launch acceptance criteria
- Approved brand logo and product photography used consistently
- Verified product details and KES prices
- Responsive and accessible shopping experience
- Real server-side inventory and checkout
- Payment provider callback/webhook verification
- Secure customer accounts, admin permissions and row-level security
- Order confirmation, delivery options, returns/privacy/terms policies
- Tested build, end-to-end flows and deployment
